Investor Eligibility

Access to opportunities presented through ZipInvest may be limited by law, jurisdiction, onboarding standards, internal risk controls, or offering-specific criteria.

Effective date: July 23, 2026 Eligibility may vary by opportunity and jurisdiction

1. General Eligibility

  • Users must be legally capable of entering binding arrangements under applicable law.
  • Users must provide complete and accurate onboarding information when requested.
  • Users must pass any identity, sanctions, KYC, AML, and fraud checks required by ZipInvest.

2. Jurisdiction Restrictions

Some opportunities may not be offered in all states, territories, or countries. ZipInvest may restrict access based on location, nationality, residency, tax profile, regulatory classification, or cross-border limitations.

3. Offering-Specific Criteria

Individual opportunities may have minimum investment amounts, qualification thresholds, document requirements, lock periods, suitability expectations, or additional disclosures. Meeting general website access requirements does not guarantee eligibility for any specific opportunity.

4. Suitability and Internal Review

ZipInvest may consider factors such as profile completeness, source-of-funds clarity, risk profile, prior compliance history, transaction pattern, or operational fit before enabling participation.

5. Right to Decline or Limit Access

ZipInvest may decline, suspend, or limit access to any user or opportunity where eligibility is not established, documents are incomplete, compliance concerns exist, or legal or operational requirements are not satisfied.

Eligibility determinations are context-specific and may change as regulations, offerings, or internal controls evolve.
