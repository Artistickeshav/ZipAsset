KYC / AML Policy

ZipInvest may require identity verification, source-of-funds review, sanctions screening, and related compliance checks before onboarding a user, accepting funds, or supporting participation in any investment-related process.

Effective date: July 23, 2026 Applies to investors, partners, and counterparties

1. Verification Requirements

ZipInvest may request identification documents, address records, tax details, business registration materials, beneficial ownership information, bank account evidence, and any other documents reasonably required to verify identity, authority, and compliance status.

2. Source of Funds and Source of Wealth

Where appropriate, users may be asked to explain or document the origin of invested funds, business income, ownership structures, or other financial background relevant to a compliance review.

3. Screening and Monitoring

  • Users may be screened against sanctions, watchlists, politically exposed person lists, and adverse media sources.
  • Activity may be reviewed for unusual patterns, inconsistent information, or indicators of fraud or unlawful conduct.
  • Enhanced diligence may be applied where higher-risk circumstances are identified.

4. Refusal, Restriction, or Suspension

ZipInvest may refuse onboarding, delay activation, request further documents, suspend access, decline transactions, or report matters to relevant authorities where required by law or where compliance concerns remain unresolved.

5. Ongoing Obligations

Users must provide accurate information and promptly update ZipInvest if identity, beneficial ownership, contact details, residency, tax status, or authorized signatory information changes.

6. Record Keeping

Verification records, compliance checks, transaction history, and supporting materials may be retained for as long as required by law, internal controls, audit, fraud prevention, or dispute handling.
