Risk Disclosure

Clean energy and mobility infrastructure investments — including EV charging networks, battery energy storage, passenger fleets, and cargo logistics — involve material business, financial, operational, regulatory, and liquidity risks. You should review risks carefully before relying on any platform information or making an investment decision.

Effective date: July 23, 2026 Applies to all platform opportunities and projections

1. No Assured Returns

Any yield illustrations, estimated cash flows, lock periods, utilization assumptions, or asset-level return ranges shown on ZipInvest are indicative only. They are not guaranteed, may change without notice, and may differ materially from actual outcomes.

2. Asset and Deployment Risk

  • Infrastructure hardware (chargers, storage systems, fleet assets) may suffer defects, downtime, or maintenance interruptions.
  • Project deployment may face delay, cost escalation, contractor issues, or site readiness constraints.
  • Energy supply, connectivity, permits, utility approvals, and operational dependencies may affect asset performance.

3. Demand and Utilization Risk

EV adoption, driver behavior, charging patterns, fleet utilization, energy consumption, storage efficiency, local competition, and market dynamics may affect throughput, occupancy, pricing power, and payback periods. Lower than expected utilization can reduce returns or impair viability.

4. Regulatory and Policy Risk

Applicable laws, tax treatment, incentive structures, EV policy, grid tariffs, urban planning rules, and sector-specific compliance expectations may change over time. Such changes may affect project economics, investor rights, disclosures, or platform operations.

5. Liquidity and Exit Risk

Investments tied to infrastructure assets may be illiquid. Exit opportunities may be limited, delayed, priced below expectation, or unavailable when desired.

6. Counterparty and Operational Risk

Outcomes may depend on performance by operators, site hosts, vendors, EPC partners, software providers, maintenance teams, collection partners, and other counterparties. Counterparty failure can impair revenues or delay recovery.

7. Concentration Risk

Exposure to a single site, city, host category, charger class, or project stage can increase risk. Diversification does not eliminate risk but may reduce dependence on any one outcome.

8. Independent Review

Prospective investors should independently evaluate suitability, risk tolerance, liquidity needs, and regulatory or tax implications before making commitments.
